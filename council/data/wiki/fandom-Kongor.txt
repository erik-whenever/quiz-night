'''Kongor''' and '''Hell Kongor''' are the Boss [[Neutral]]s of [[Forests of Caldavar]]. They are the toughest NPC units in the map, and killing them gives the team a significant advantage, and their deaths are announced on the map.

Kongor and Hell Kongor are located near the ancient camps on each side, with Kongor on the southeast side and Hell Kongor on the northwest side. Both are identical in terms of stats and abilities.

==Abilities==
In addition to these abilities, every 30 seconds while active, Kongor and Hell Kongor gain 1 Damage, 50 Max Health, 0.15 Armor, and 3 bonus damage to Slam. Bonuses stop accumulating at 120 charges, or 60 minutes.

==Mechanics==
''The following also applies to Hell Kongor.''
===Spawning===
Kongor is invulnerable and hidden until 6:00.

When Kongor is killed, he will respawn exactly 8 minutes after his death. It is impossible to prevent Kongor from spawning.

===Lair===
Kongor cannot be attacked from outside his lair. Any attacks attempted from outside his lair will show a pop-up as a Miss, but [[True Strike]] will not allow you to damage Kongor from outside his lair. However, abilities will damage Kongor, regardless damage type or whether the user is inside or outside the lair.

===AI===
If there are 3 or more units with 230 radius of Kongor, Kongor will cast [[#Slam|Slam]].

If Kongor is lured outside of his lair for more than 3 seconds, he will be forced to return to his lair. When this happens, any debuffs are dispelled from Kongor, and Kongor gains Invulnerability, Cliffwalking and Buildingwalking until he returns to his lair.

Kongor will never attack Towers.

===Bounty===
When Kongor is killed, the killer will receive 350 gold. In addition, the killer's teammates will receive 200 gold. The killer does not receive this gold. If a non-player controlled unit lands the killing blow on Kongor, it gives 230 gold to the team instead.

Killing Kongor grants each player on the killer's team a permanent stat bonus. They can choose from the following:
* '''Vitality''': +100 Max Health, +50 Max Mana, +2% Healing Received, +2 Health Regeneration, +0.6 Mana Regeneration
* '''Damage''': +4% Damage Dealt
* '''Speed''': +10 Movement Speed
These effects can stack.

In addition, the team will receive the '''Kongor Slayer''' buff for 3 minutes and restore 40% of Max Health and 20% of Max Mana over 10 seconds (removed on taking damage from enemy players). Gaining the '''Kongor Slayer''' buff while you already have the buff will grant the '''Kongor Decimator''' buff for 3 minutes, granting bonus damage vs. structures (starts at +60%, which increases linearly starting from the 20 minute mark, up to +80% at the 45 minute mark; halved in Mid Wars) and restores 80% of Max Health and 40% of Max Mana over 10 seconds (removed on taking damage from enemy players).

If a team kills Kongor or Hell Kongor with the '''Kongor Slayer''' buff active (in other words, kills both Kongors within 3 minutes), they will also drop a {{ii|Token of Life}}.
